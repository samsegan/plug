The first time

  1. Open Plug.dmg and drag Plug to Applications.
  2. Open Plug. macOS says it can't check it for malware (it isn't notarized yet): click Done.
  3. System Settings › Privacy & Security › scroll down › Open Anyway next to Plug.

After that Plug updates itself: it checks every few hours and installs a new version when you quit it.
